# AURA safety and platform limits

- AURA is not an emergency service and does not dispatch police, ambulance, or a staffed response team.
- Android detection runs through a visible microphone foreground service after informed permission. Battery-optimization guidance and accessibility shortcuts are opt-in.
- iOS supports local recognition only while the operating system permits it. It does not promise autonomous SMS, volume-key controls, or persistent background audio detection.
- A qualifying model prediction starts a 20-second cancellation window; only a server-side incident dispatch sends trusted-contact alerts.
- Ambient audio is never retained by default. Optional false-alert donations are encrypted, explicitly consented, and separately governed.
