# Dataset acceptance policy

1. The manifest generator never marks a clip commercially eligible.
2. A reviewer records source, exact licence, attribution requirement, and provenance before enabling a clip.
3. Training, validation, and test partitions must be source-disjoint where possible. Segments derived from one source recording must stay in one partition.
4. Non-commercial, unknown, or revoked material is excluded from production training and release evaluation.
5. Every release report includes its manifest hash, model hash, label distribution, per-class precision/recall, false-alert rate, and slice results.
